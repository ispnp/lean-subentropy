# Theorem 1 and its Lean challenge

The table compares [Section 1.2 of *Quantum Subentropy: Subadditivity and Sharp Bounds on Discrimination Error*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30220v1#S1.SS2) with [the challenge](Challenge.lean). The last column links to proofs in the paper's Lean companion.

The challenge represents the labels by `Fin n` and the signal space by complex matrices indexed by a finite basis `B`. Each signal is positive semidefinite with trace one. The priors are nonnegative and sum to one. Thus zero priors, singular states, and mixed signals are included. Relabeling identifies any finite alphabet of size $n$ with `Fin n`.

The minimum formulation explains the quantifier in “strongest universal lower bound.” Fix $n$ and an admissible $q$. If $L(q)$ bounds the error from below for every ensemble with this conditional subentropy, it also bounds the error of the attaining ensemble. Consequently $L(q)\leq\Phi_n^{-1}(q)$ pointwise, without any monotonicity or continuity assumption on $L$.
