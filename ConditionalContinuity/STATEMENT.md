# Sharp trace-distance continuity bounds for conditional subentropy

The table compares Theorem 14 and Corollary 15 of *Quantum Subentropy: Subadditivity and Sharp Bounds on Discrimination Error* with the nine Lean declarations in this entry. [Section 4.2 of the paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30220v1#S4.SS2) · [Challenge.lean](Challenge.lean)

The labels are indexed by `Fin n` and a basis for the quantum system by `Fin m`. A state consists of positive semidefinite blocks whose traces sum to one. Their direct sum gives the joint state, and their sum gives its quantum marginal. This representation includes zero blocks and singular marginals. The trace distance $D$ is half the sum of the absolute eigenvalues of the Hermitian joint difference. The challenge uses the determinant integral in equation (27) for $K$ and the Euclidean operator norm for $\kappa$. The companion also proves the Gaussian representation in that equation. [Definitions](Challenge.lean#L22) · [Gaussian representation](https://pratikpatil.io/lean/subentropy/source/gaussianspatialsign#L46)

Write $\Delta Q$ for the absolute difference in conditional subentropy between the two states.

## How sharpness is witnessed

For the global coefficient, fix the two dimensions and a pure signal. Compare the priors $(1,0)$ and $(1-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ on that signal, padding with zero labels as needed. Their joint distance is $\varepsilon$, and $\Delta Q/\varepsilon=\Phi_2(\varepsilon)/\varepsilon$ tends to one as $\varepsilon$ decreases to zero. Thus every smaller coefficient fails even at arbitrarily small positive distance. Every distinct pair still satisfies the strict inequality above. [Global sharpness witnesses](https://pratikpatil.io/lean/subentropy/source/cqoptimalcoefficient#L35)

For the fixed-marginal coefficient, start with blocks $(\tau,0,\ldots)$ and transfer $\varepsilon P$ from the first block to the second. Here $P$ projects onto a common eigenvector of $\tau$ and $K(\tau)$ in the support of $\tau$, chosen for the largest eigenvalue of $K(\tau)$. The marginal stays equal to $\tau$ throughout. The distance is $\varepsilon$, and the ratio tends to $\kappa(\tau)$. This construction also works when $\tau$ is singular. [Fixed-marginal sharpness witnesses](https://pratikpatil.io/lean/subentropy/source/fixedmarginalcontinuity#L24)
